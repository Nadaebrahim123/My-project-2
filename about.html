About Planning

A good daily plan helps you manage your time and balance studying, activities, and rest.

Simple Planning Tips

  • Write down your daily tasks.
  • Choose your most important tasks first.
  • Set a specific time for studying.
  • Take regular breaks.
  • Review your plan at the end of the day.
